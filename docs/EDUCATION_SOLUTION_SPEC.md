# Education solution experience — initial specification

## Purpose and scope
Help school and college leaders, education providers, learners and guardians explore a proposed digital solution before discussing a written scope. The industry page covers schools, colleges, madrasas, coaching, education consultancy, English preparation, university admission preparation and job exam preparation. It is an illustrative service experience, not a directory of verified institutions or proof that modules exist in production.

## Page and navigation
The Education and Schools & Training industry pages include a visual grid. Each keyboard-operable card scrolls to its corresponding detailed section without altering the site's hash-based route. Each detail presents an audience, overview, proposed programme/service content, organisational structure, facilities and distinctive features, and an illustrative workflow. Contact remains the existing contact route. Images are AI-generated representative illustrations, not photographs of client institutions.

## College profile: information requirements
A real college page would require institution-approved name and identity; programme/department names, entry requirements and calendar; governance, leadership and contacts; verified labs, library, accessibility, student services and other facilities; distinctive features; current admission information and source/last-reviewed date. No such institution-specific claims are currently populated. Publication, edits, access and consent must be authorised by the institution; avoid exposing sensitive student records.

## Proposed operational workflow
Visitor explores a profile and programme, submits an enquiry through an approved channel, staff review and respond, and any application proceeds under a separately agreed admissions process. Attendance, progress, notices, fees and other modules are scope candidates, not live capabilities. No availability, outcomes, automated messaging, payment handling or admission decisions are implied by the design.

## Next implementation gates
Inventory existing source and licensed templates; establish tenant and object isolation, staff roles, audit trail, consent and retention; define institution-owned content and approval/versioning; verify accessibility and mobile layouts; implement and test real workflows against a non-production environment; obtain release approval before deployment. Never publish a client profile, student information or institutional claims without authorised evidence.

## Detailed content model and publication requirements
- **Overview:** institution-provided identity, mission, ownership, authorised contact and last-reviewed date. Do not create or infer registration, affiliation, outcomes or rankings.
- **Academic programmes:** approved qualification/grade, department, subject, delivery mode, duration, eligibility, calendar, fees and application information; each field is optional until confirmed and should show its review status.
- **Organisation:** institution-approved governing body, principal, departments, office responsibilities and official contacts; maintain role-based edit and publish permissions.
- **Facilities and distinctiveness:** verified laboratories, library, accessibility, transport, extracurricular activities, student services and approved media with permission and provenance. No unverified capacity or availability.
- **Future plans:** institution-authored goals with an explicit proposed/approved distinction and review date. No promise of funding, approval or opening dates without evidence.
- **Operational flows:** public visitor reads an authorised profile → selects a programme → contacts the institution or submits a consent-based enquiry → staff triage and follow up within their approved workflow. Student, admissions and guardian data must not be exposed publicly.
- **Governance:** drafts and revisions need authorised institutional ownership, human approval and publication audit; privacy, retention, access control and deletion processes must be established before collecting real data. Public college records and backend publication workflows are not implemented by the present illustrative page.

## Current implementation and gaps (2026-10-31)
The industry directory covers 20 sectors with locally hosted AI-generated representative images; several sectors intentionally share an image. Education uses eight illustrated scope cards, each scrolling to programme, organisation, facilities and workflow descriptions. These are conceptual prompts, not profiles of actual schools or colleges. There is no institution-specific database, authenticated editing, publishing process, or verified campus information yet. Institution owners must supply and approve those details. Mobile/browser rendering and end-to-end enquiry flow remain unverified.
